# Loka <> PHV Focus — Deep Dive #1 — 2026-04-23

## Key Takeaways
- Project codename: Metis. Vision is to democratize healthspan through scalable, white-labeled benchmarking software for senior living
- Core architecture: segregated data silos per operator with centralized insights; two apps (resident = Focus Forward, staff = Focus Activate)
- Buck Institute engine will be integrated within the Focus environment for personalized, science-backed health recommendations
- System requires internal medical review guardrails to manage recommendation logic and liability
- Resident app is the primary deliverable; staff app second

## Decisions Made
- Buck Institute engine will be integrated, not rebuilt — Loka builds the integration layer
- Architecture supports multi-tenant, white-labeled deployment
- Medical review guardrails required before any health recommendations are surfaced to residents
- Development will begin immediately with two-week sprints
